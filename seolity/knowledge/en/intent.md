# Matching search intent and SERP formats

## Reading the SERP
- Dominant result type tells you the expected format: product grids → category page; long guides → article; comparison tables → "vs" article; videos → video content; local pack → local landing page + Business Profile.
- SERP features to note: featured snippet (answer in 40–60 words or a list/table), People Also Ask (add concise answers to related questions), image pack (optimize images), shopping/merchant listings (product structured data), AI overviews (clear, well-structured, citable facts).

## Aligning a page with intent
- Informational: answer first, then explain; include steps, definitions, visuals; link to commercial pages where it helps.
- Commercial investigation: criteria, comparisons, pros/cons, recommendations by use case, transparent methodology.
- Transactional: price, availability, shipping/returns, specifications, reviews, clear CTA, fast page.
- Local: address, hours, map, service area, local proof.

## Intent mismatch symptoms
- Impressions without clicks for a query class, high bounce from search, rankings that fluctuate between your page and different page types. Fix by changing format or targeting a different query, not by adding keywords.
