# Building topic clusters and pillar pages

## Steps
1. Collect queries and existing URLs for a theme.
2. Group by meaning and intent (queries that the same page could satisfy belong together).
3. Choose a pillar per group: the broad, comprehensive page (often a category or ultimate guide).
4. Map supporting pages to specific subtopics; mark missing subtopics as content to create.
5. Interlink: each supporting page links to the pillar with descriptive anchors; the pillar links to every supporting page; related supporting pages link to each other where useful.
6. Keep URL structure and breadcrumbs consistent with the cluster hierarchy.

## Quality checks
- Each page has a unique purpose and primary keyword.
- The pillar is reachable within 2–3 clicks from the homepage.
- No orphan supporting pages (pages with zero internal inlinks).
- Clusters for e-commerce: category (pillar) ↔ buying guides, comparisons, how-to/care articles ↔ product pages.
