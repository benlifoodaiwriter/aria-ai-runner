# Keyword research, search intent and keyword mapping

## Sources of keyword ideas
- Your own Search Console queries (most reliable: real impressions for your site), internal site search, customer questions, support tickets, product attributes.
- Autocomplete, People Also Ask, related searches, competitor pages and categories.
- Classify keywords as head (short, broad, competitive), body, and long-tail (specific, lower volume, usually higher conversion).

## Search intent
- Informational ("how to descale an espresso machine"), commercial investigation ("best espresso machine for home", "X vs Y"), transactional ("buy Breville 870 price"), navigational ("brand login").
- The SERP is the ground truth: look at what currently ranks (guides, category pages, product pages, videos) and match the format.
- One page should target one primary intent. Mixed intents usually need separate pages.

## Keyword mapping
- Assign each keyword cluster to exactly one target URL. Two URLs targeting the same cluster cause cannibalization.
- Category/collection pages target broad transactional terms; product pages target model-specific terms; blog guides target informational and commercial investigation terms and link to the transactional pages.
- Record for each mapping: primary keyword, secondary variants, intent, target URL, current position, opportunity.

## Prioritization
- Opportunity ≈ demand × achievable position gain × business value ÷ effort.
- Quick wins: queries ranking 4–20 with meaningful impressions ("striking distance"), pages with low CTR vs expected, and new queries rising week over week.
- Difficulty depends on the authority and relevance of current top results; be realistic for new sites.

## Rules for AI keyword suggestions
- Suggest keywords in the site's language and market, with natural phrasing users actually type (including colloquial forms).
- Never fabricate search volumes; express opportunity qualitatively unless real data is supplied.
