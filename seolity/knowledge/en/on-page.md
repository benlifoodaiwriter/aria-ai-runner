# On-page elements checklist

## Elements and rules
- Title: unique, descriptive, primary topic first, ≈ 600 px.
- Meta description: unique summary with concrete value, ≈ 120–160 chars.
- H1: one per page, matches the topic; H2/H3 reflect subtopics and questions.
- URL slug: short, descriptive, hyphenated; don't change existing URLs without a 301.
- Introduction: states what the page offers and answers the main question quickly.
- Body: covers the intent comprehensively with original information; uses lists/tables where helpful.
- Media: relevant images/videos with alt text and captions.
- Links: descriptive internal links to related pages; external links to authoritative sources where they help the reader.
- Structured data: matches visible content.
- Freshness: update facts and dates when content changes.

## Common problems the audit flags
- Missing/duplicate/too long titles and descriptions, missing or multiple H1, thin content, missing alt, broken links, noindex on important pages, canonical pointing elsewhere, slow pages, missing structured data, orphan pages.
