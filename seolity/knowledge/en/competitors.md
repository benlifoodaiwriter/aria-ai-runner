# Competitor analysis and content gap

## Who your search competitors are
- Sites that rank for your target queries, which may differ from business competitors (marketplaces, publishers, forums).

## What to analyze
- Topic and page coverage (sitemaps, categories), page types that win (guides, tools, comparison pages), content depth and freshness, structured data use, internal linking patterns, title/description patterns, speed and UX.
- Patterns in why they rank: better match to intent, more comprehensive answers, first-hand evidence, stronger authority.

## Turning findings into actions
- Gaps: topics they cover that you don't → create original pages if relevant to your audience.
- Weaknesses: thin or outdated competitor pages → an opportunity for a clearly better resource.
- Strengths to learn from: formats and UX patterns (comparison tables, calculators, filters).

## Ethics
- Crawl politely (respect robots.txt, rate limits), never copy text or images, and never use competitor trademarks deceptively.
