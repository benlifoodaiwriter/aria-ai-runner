# SERP analysis and AI search visibility

## Analyzing a results page
- Identify dominant intent and content format, the SERP features present, the type of sites ranking (brands, marketplaces, publishers, forums) and the angle of top results (freshness, price, beginner-friendly).
- Find the "information gain" opportunity: what useful information is missing in current top results that you can genuinely provide?

## Featured snippets and People Also Ask
- Provide a direct, self-contained answer right after a question-style heading (≈ 40–60 words), or a clean list/table for steps and comparisons. Expand afterward.

## AI overviews and AI assistants
- There are no special optimizations required beyond standard SEO: pages must be indexable and eligible to show a snippet. Clear structure, accurate facts, original insights and a trustworthy source make content more likely to be cited.
- Use nosnippet/max-snippet controls only if you deliberately want to limit how content is shown.
