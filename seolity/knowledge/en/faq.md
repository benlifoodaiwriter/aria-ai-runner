# Writing FAQ sections

## When to add FAQs
- When users have recurring, specific questions related to the page (from People Also Ask, Search Console queries, support, reviews). Not as filler on every page.

## How to write them
- Questions phrased the way users ask them; answers short (1–3 sentences), factual and complete on their own, with a link to deeper content if needed.
- Answers must be consistent with the page and the business's real policies (shipping, warranty, returns). Never invent policies, prices or guarantees.
- 3–8 questions are usually enough; avoid duplicate questions across many pages.

## Markup
- FAQPage JSON-LD must match the visible FAQ text exactly. Google shows FAQ rich results only for authoritative government and health sites, so the main value for most sites is user satisfaction and clear, citable answers.
